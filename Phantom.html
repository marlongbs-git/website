SFU Team Phantom Formula SAE Electric

Vehicle Dynamics: driver safety systems

Team Phantom is SFU's Formula SAE Electric team, where students across disciplines design and build a race car that must pass FSAE's strict safety rules.

On the Vehicle Dynamics subteam, I worked on driver safety systems: seat positioning, seat brackets, and harness mounting tabs, all to FSAE rules.

  • Role Vehicle Dynamics member, driver safety systems
  • Team SFU Team Phantom, Formula SAE Electric
  • When September 2023 - May 2025
  • Tools SolidWorks, ANSYS Mechanical

Highlights

  • Designed and manufactured seat brackets that fit drivers from 152–187 cm and 49–102 kg while meeting FSAE ergonomic and safety rules
  • Simulated and optimized harness mounting tabs in ANSYS Mechanical to meet the 15 kN pullout requirement
  • Wrote a harness test plan and designed test fixtures to validate the FEA with physical load tests
  • Managed SolidWorks assemblies of 80+ components, keeping models consistent across design iterations

Design Process

1. Fitting a legacy seat into a new frame

This was one of my first large design teams. My task was to fit an existing seat into a newly built frame, working around parts that couldn't change, in a tight space, while keeping a safe margin on every part.

I started by recreating fitment models from previous years to understand the space available, then redesigned the seat bracket to fit the new chassis geometry.

2. Verifying the harness tabs with FEA

Fresh off a mechanical design and FEA course, I applied it directly. I kept element quality consistent across the mesh, minimized pyramid elements, and kept skewness low, then applied bearing loads to the harness tabs and checked them against the competition rules.

The biggest lesson was the difference between running a simulation and running one where the physics actually makes sense. I've carried that into every project since.

Skills Used

Finite Element Analysis

Static structural analysis of driver safety parts in ANSYS Mechanical against FSAE load cases.

Mechanical Design

Brackets, frame tubing, harness tabs, and driver fitment models.

Large Assemblies

Integrating parts from multiple subteams to check fit within the chassis and vehicle envelope.
